The churn wound

When a member cancels,
the reason walks out the door with them.

Patreon, YouTube Memberships, Substack, Discord — churn is the direct revenue wound, and nobody writes down why. Months later the creator fixes the very thing that drove people away… and the people who left for exactly that reason are never told.

Today's entire win-back industry
“Hey… we miss you 💔 Here's 20% off.”
to: everyone · when: whenever · why they left: unknown
✗ ignores why each person actually left
Make the pain specific: the reason is spoken once — in a cancel box or a Discord grumble — and then it evaporates. So the only tool anyone has is the generic guilt-blast. It goes to everyone, at a random time, and it converts terribly because it has no idea why any individual person left.
The insight

Message the right people the moment their specific reason stops being true.

Win-back is a timing-and-memory problem, not a discount problem. That takes an agent that remembers the reason and can judge the fix.

The unlock is precision in both dimensions: WHO (only the members whose stated reason is now resolved) and WHEN (the exact moment it's resolved). No stateless tool can do this — it requires memory of the reason and semantic judgement about the fix. That's the whole product on one line.
What Porchlight does // one flow, done deep

Memory. Meaning. Autonomy.

01 · MEMORY

Exit interview on cancel

The Mind runs a short, warm interview and files a structured return-condition — reason, verbatim quote, do-not-contact — into per-member long-term memory. One Mind conversation per member: the memory is the Mind's, not a database row.

02 · SEMANTIC MATCH

Judged by meaning

When the creator announces a change, the Mind asks of every open reason: “Does this genuinely resolve THIS member's reason? Judge by meaning, not keywords.” Only the resolved get contacted.

03 · AUTONOMOUS WIN-BACK

Unprompted, in their words

The Mind drafts and sends personal win-backs quoting each member's own words from weeks earlier — and honors do-not-contact even when the reason is fixed. Restraint is shown on the dashboard, not buried.

Every departure compounds into a churn board — 37% of 19 seeded departures left over the pivot away from deep-dives
